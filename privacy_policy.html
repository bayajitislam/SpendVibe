SpendVibe ("we", "our", or "the App") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how information is handled when you use the SpendVibe mobile application distributed through the Google Play Store.

1. Summary: 100% Offline Architecture

SpendVibe operates under a strict offline-first policy:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to register, create a profile, or submit your email address.
  • No Financial Credentials: SpendVibe never accesses, links to, or requests your bank credentials or card information.
  • Exclusively Local Storage: All records, categories, transaction amounts, and budgets reside strictly on your physical device via an internal SQLite database.
  • No Server Collection: We do not operate external web servers to monitor, collect, synchronize, or monetize your financial activity.

2. Information Collection and Handling

  • Personal Information: We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identifiable information (including name, physical address, email, telephone number, or device identifiers).
  • Financial Records: Income, expenses, category assignments, and budget limits entered by the user are stored locally on device storage. This data is never accessible to us.
  • Device Permissions: SpendVibe does not request dangerous Android permissions (such as Location, Camera, Contacts, Microphone, SMS, or Phone State).

3. Data Ownership and Deletion

Users maintain complete ownership over their financial records:

  • CSV Data Export: You can export your full transaction records at any time as a CSV file using the in-app export utility.
  • Data Deletion: You can permanently delete all local financial data at any time by clearing application data via your device settings (Settings > Apps > SpendVibe > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-Party Integrations

SpendVibe does not contain third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services in this version. When exporting CSV files, the application interfaces with your operating system's native share feature to deliver files to the destination you select (such as Google Drive or Email).

5. Children's Privacy

SpendVibe does not address or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

6. Security

Because all data is retained directly on your mobile device, the security of your stored records is protected by your device's operating system security and access controls (PIN, password, or biometrics).
