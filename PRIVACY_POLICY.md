## 1. Summary: 100% Offline and Private

SpendVibe is designed with an **offline-first philosophy**:
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide an email address.
- **No Bank Credentials**: SpendVibe never asks for, accesses, or links to your bank accounts, credit cards, or financial institutions.
- **Local Storage Only**: All financial records, income and expense transactions, custom notes, and budget limits remain stored exclusively on your physical device in a local SQLite database.
- **Zero Server Transmissions**: We do not operate external servers to collect, upload, monitor, or sell your personal or financial data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
SpendVibe **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, physical address, or device IP address).

### B. Financial Data
All transaction entries (amounts, categories, titles, notes, and dates) and monthly budget configurations you enter into the App are saved **strictly locally** on your device storage. We cannot access, view, or recover this information.

### C. Device Permissions
SpendVibe does not request or require sensitive Android permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or Phone State).

---

## 3. Data Portability & User Control

You maintain complete ownership and control over your financial records:
- **CSV Data Export**: You can export your full transaction history into a standard CSV spreadsheet format at any time using the in-app Export function.
- **Data Deletion**: You can permanently remove all stored financial data at any time by clearing the App's storage via your device settings (`Settings > Apps > SpendVibe > Clear Storage`) or by uninstalling the App.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Links

The current version of SpendVibe operates independently without third-party tracking, profiling, or analytical telemetry. When using the CSV Export feature, the App interacts with your operating system's native share sheet to allow you to send your export file to destinations of your choice (such as Google Drive, Email, or file managers).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

SpendVibe does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. Because the App collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children.

---

## 6. Security

Because all data remains on your physical device, the security of your information relies on your device's operating system security features. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock (PIN, password, or biometrics).
